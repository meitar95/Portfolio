Decision 1 of 4 · Structure

28 fields → 8 families

Ruled out

A big side panel

Most fields don’t apply to most users.

Ruled out

One flat list

Everyone scans all 28.

Chose

A dropdown, in 8 families.

10–14 fields per customer, not 28.

Decision 2 of 4 · Visibility

Every filter in sight, in plain words

Ruled out

A collapsed filter row

Hidden filters changed the results.

Ruled out

All-in-one panel

Tested against inline. Inline won.

Chose

Visible chips. Presets in their own row.

Decision 3 of 4 · Exclusion

Make “is not” visible, one click away

Ruled out

Negate on hover

Hidden until hover. Easy to miss.

Ruled out

Include / exclude columns

Two lists per field. Too heavy.

Chose

Click “is” to flip it.

Rebuilt from the working prototype. Demo data.
